Who We Are

Cholai Tech is Papua New Guinea's first AI-powered technology company. Founded in 2026 and based in beautiful Manus Island + Western Highlands, we build digital solutions that work for PNG people, on PNG internet, with PNG languages.

PNG: 9 Million People | 800+ Languages | Only 11% Online
That means 8 Million people are waiting for their first digital opportunity.

We build for PNG first. If it works in Lae on a K50 phone with Digicel data, it'll work anywhere. We understand village internet speeds, power cuts, and how business really works here.

Why We Exist

Connectivity is arriving in PNG through fiber and satellites like Starlink. But technology without purpose is useless.

Cholai Tech exists to do 2 things:

1. Archive PNG: Protect our cultures, traditions, tokples languages, and stories before they disappear.

2. Grow PNG: Give every business, creator, and community the tools to earn online and compete globally.
