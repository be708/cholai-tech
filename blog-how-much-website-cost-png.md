# How Much Does a Website Cost in PNG in 2026?

If you're a business owner in Port Moresby, Lae, or anywhere in PNG, you've probably asked this question. 

The short answer: **It depends on what you need.**

## 1. Basic Website: K500 - K1500
**Best for:** Small shops, cafes, personal brands

What you get:
- 1-5 pages: Home, About, Services, Contact
- Mobile-friendly design
- Contact form + WhatsApp button
- Basic Google setup

## 2. Business Website: K2000 - K5000
**Best for:** Companies, schools, NGOs

What you get:
- 5-10 pages
- Professional design + logo
- Blog/news section
- Google Search Console + Analytics setup
- SEO to show up on Google

## 3. E-commerce / Custom Website: K5000+
**Best for:** Shops selling online, big companies

What you get:
- Online store + payments
- Customer login system
- Custom features
- Full marketing setup
